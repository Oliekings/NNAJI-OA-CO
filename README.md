# NNAJI O.A & COMPANY — Corporate Real Estate Platform

An enterprise real estate and statutory valuation management platform engineered for **NNAJI O.A & COMPANY** (Estate Surveyors & Valuers, Est. 1981 — Recognized by NIESV, ESVRBON & CASLE).

---

## 🏛 Practice Overview
- **Cadastral & Asset Valuation**: Statutory appraisals, compensation assessments, balance sheet valuations, and plant & machinery inventory.
- **Facility & Estate Management**: Multi-unit commercial high-rises, residential estates, and industrial complexes.
- **Agency & Investment Appraisal**: Property acquisition, disposals, joint-venture partnerships, and feasibility studies.

---

## 🛠 Technology Stack
- **Framework**: [Laravel 13](https://laravel.com) (PHP 8.3+)
- **Database**: MySQL / PostgreSQL / SQLite
- **Architecture**: MVC with custom Repository/Service patterns
- **Styling**: Google Stitch Design System tokens (Forest Green, Warm Gold, Ivory) with responsive Vanilla CSS and Tailwind utilities
- **Testing**: PHPUnit / Feature test suites

---

## 🚀 Key Platform Features
1. **Property Lifecycle Automation**: Real-time transition between active market listings and historical closed transaction portfolios.
2. **Statutory Valuation Mandate Portal**: Multi-step client brief and valuation inquiry intake system.
3. **Interactive High-Resolution Dossier Viewer**: Full-screen image lightbox with multi-axis zoom, drag-to-pan, and multi-photo gallery cycling.
4. **Secure Image Processing & Optimization**: Server-side binary validation, EXIF metadata sanitization, and automatic conversion to compressed WebP.
5. **Administrative CMS**: Secure management for properties, service areas, executive leadership, and incoming corporate inquiries.

---

## 🔒 Security Architecture
- Route-level rate limiting (`throttle`) against brute-force and submission spam.
- Strict MIME and binary image verification preventing unauthorized script execution.
- CSRF protection across all forms and endpoints.
- Parameterized ORM queries with Eloquent mass-assignment guards.
- Protected administrative endpoints without public trace.
